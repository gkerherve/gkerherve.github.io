# The Realistic style's photographs

Every photograph here is **CC0** — public domain, free to use, change and ship,
no attribution required. They are credited anyway, because they are somebody's
work and because a block is easier to fix when you know where it came from.

Each block material is baked from one source asset by
`tools/bake_real_textures.py` (colour with the occlusion baked in, and normal +
roughness packed into `_nr.jpg`), at 256 texels a block.
