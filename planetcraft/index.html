you are a chief

The aim is the planet. Grow a settlement, earn gold, grow its people, get on with the neighbours — every settlement under one chief, and then the other planets.

found a village of your own

  1. Craft a bell — three planks over four bronze ingots — and place it in wild country, well clear of any settlement. The ground around it is yours from that moment, named and on the books.
  2. Ring the bell with coin in your pocket and a settler answers — 30 coin each, men and women in turn.
  3. Build houses and lay beds — every bed near your bell makes room for one more soul. At night, couples pair and children are born.
  4. Point at a villager and press Z. The first press takes them on — a meal out of your pack and 24 coin — and every press after it is the order card: cut wood, farm, mine, cook, build. A villager who is not yours yet takes no orders, which is why the hiring is on the same key.

take another village

A village that swears to you keeps its bell, its people and its chief, sends you a share of its takings every dawn, and its people take your orders with Z. There are two roads to that oath:

  1. The slow one — goodwill. Every coin you spend or earn at a village's counter makes it think better of you; so does a gift to its chief, and every wrong undoes it. When it thinks well enough of you, right-click its chief and say “Swear to me”: for a purse of coin he will — and it is his own decision, made by the mind that runs the villages.
  2. The fast one — the sword. Hold a sword, spear, hammer or bow and right-click one of your hired hands to arm him; Z on an armed hand, then raid. Hold the village's fields with its garrison broken and it yields, a quarter of its purse with it.
  3. Or empty it: bring its bell down and its people leave; a bell of your own in the empty belfry makes the houses yours.
  4. The other chiefs are doing the same to each other. A chief who wins a raid takes the loser's oath, and realms grow — beat the head of one and everything sworn to it comes with it.

the planet, and the other planets

The line at the top of the screen says which ring you are on, and J → the road shows every village and who it answers to. Hold every settlement and the world is yours: its bells ring, and it sends its tribute after you wherever you go. Then press L for the next world — the hands standing with you at the pad come too, arms and all.

earning coin

Sell goods over any village shop counter, or mine gold (it lives deep in the rock) and strike it at a mint — nine coin to the bar.
