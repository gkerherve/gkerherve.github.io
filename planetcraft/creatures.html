Make one

  1. In KherveCAD: Library ▸ Toys & models ▸ Monsters has a troll, a dragon, a spider and a dozen more, ready to walk. Change it however you like.
  2. Or model your own, and name its pieces: Head, Tail, Left wing / Right wing, and each leg — Left leg, Front right leg… A named piece is a joint: legs swing at the top, the head nods at its neck.
  3. File ▸ Send to PlanetCraft… and say what it is: an animal, a person, or a monster that hunts you.
  4. It walks up to you here, and appears in this book.
With Claude in KherveCAD, just ask — “make me a troll for PlanetCraft”: it has build_creature and send_to_planetcraft.
